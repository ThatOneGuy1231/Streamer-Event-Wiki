About the Server

Imperial Topaz is a Cobblemon SMP -- a Minecraft server built around Cobblemon, with a modpack layered on top for trainer battles, breeding, storage, and quality-of-life, plus a small player-run economy and tools for streamers to capture and share moments from the server.

  • Trainer battles, breeding, and a modpack full of Pokémon-focused fixes and animations
  • A small player-run economy -- shops, a casino, and more
  • Streamer tools like cutscenes and instant replays for capturing moments live
